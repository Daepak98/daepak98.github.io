## About me

Hello there! My name is Deepak Warrier, and welcome to my website!

The goal of this site is to be able to present who I am, what I am doing, and what I'm interested in. As such, you will see a few different parts of this site. 

First, you'll notice this **Home** page, which will have, what is effectively, my resume. It will include my major skills, education, and current work I'm doing. You'll also notice the **Projects** page, which will link to my current projects that I'm hosting on Github, or posts about my projects that I don't have repos to share on. And lastly, you'll notice the **Posts** down below (and they have their own page [here]()).

Please do browse around and get to learn more about me! If you every want to contact me to talk about anything, please feel free to use my contact points in the footer below.
